## Background

Henri Rousseau painted the *Portrait de Monsieur X* in 1906,
oil on canvas, 61 by 50 cm.
It is in the Kunsthaus Zürich.
It is signed "H. Rousseau" at the lower right but not dated.

The sitter has never been firmly identified.
The writer Pierre Loti is the usual suggestion,
which is why the painting is often called
*Portrait de Pierre Loti*.

Behind the man,
rows of houses run back into a hilly landscape,
four factory chimneys rise over one shoulder,
one of them smoking,
and an acacia grows over the other.
The art historian Michel Hoog read the tree and the chimneys
as wild nature against urban industry.
