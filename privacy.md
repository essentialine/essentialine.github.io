*Last updated: 2026-10-12*

This policy covers the essentialine.art website
and the tools its owner uses to publish essentialine's drawings
to YouTube and Pinterest.
essentialine is run by one person, an artist;
"we" below means that person.
Questions go to the contact address at the end.

## Summary

This site is an educational art blog.
It does not sell anything here, run its own analytics,
or set advertising cookies.
We collect only the email address of people who join the mailing list,
and we never sell or share personal data.

## What we collect and why

- **Mailing list:** your email address, if you choose to sign up.
  Used only to send posts and updates from essentialine.
- **Messages you send us:** your email address and what you write,
  used only to reply.
- **Nothing else.** There are no accounts, comments, forms or tracking on this site.
  Search runs in your browser and sends nothing to us.

## Mailing list

If you sign up for the mailing list,
your email address is stored by [ConvertKit](https://kit.com/) (the newsletter service this site uses)
and is used only to send occasional posts
and updates from essentialine.
It is never sold or shared.
Every email includes an unsubscribe link;
you can also write to the address below to be removed,
and your address will be deleted.

## Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own.
Services named below (an embedded video player, for instance)
may set their own when you use them,
under their own policies.

## Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages,
which may log standard request data (IP address, browser,
page requested) for security and operational purposes,
per [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
We do not receive or keep those logs.

## Embedded video

Some posts embed process videos hosted on Cloudflare Stream
and linked on YouTube.
Playing an embedded video loads content from those services,
which have their own privacy policies ([Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/),
[YouTube / Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

## Prints

Prints of the drawings are sold through Etsy and Redbubble,
not on this site.
A purchase there is handled under
[Etsy's](https://www.etsy.com/legal/privacy/) or
[Redbubble's](https://www.redbubble.com/privacy) privacy policy.

## YouTube

The owner publishes to the essentialine YouTube channel,
authorised through YouTube's own login.
This acts only on that channel,
and collects no data about anyone else on YouTube.

## Pinterest app

The essentialine Pinterest app
is a private tool used only by the owner of this site,
to post essentialine's own drawings to the essentialine Pinterest account.
No one else signs in to it.

What it accesses, through the Pinterest API, and why:

- **The account's username** (`user_accounts:read`),
  to confirm it is signed in to the right account.
- **Its boards** (`boards:read`, `boards:write`),
  to pick the board a drawing goes on.
- **Its own Pins** (`pins:read`),
  so a drawing is never pinned twice.
- **New Pins** (`pins:write`):
  an image, a title, a description
  and a link to the drawing's print listing.

It reads nothing about other Pinterest users,
and shares nothing with anyone.
The only thing it stores is the account's authorisation token,
kept on the owner's own computer, never on a server
or with any third party.
It can be revoked at any time in the Pinterest account's settings,
and is deleted if the app stops being used.

## How long we keep data

Mailing-list addresses are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them.
Emails are kept only as long as the conversation needs.
The Pinterest and YouTube authorisations are kept until revoked.

## Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you,
have it corrected,
or have it deleted,
by writing to the address below.
We answer within 30 days.
Depending on where you live (the EU, UK or California, for instance)
you may have further rights under local law,
and we will honour them.

## Security

We keep what little we hold to a minimum.
Credentials stay on the owner's encrypted computer,
and the site itself is served over HTTPS.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13,
and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
If you believe a child has signed up to the mailing list,
write to us and the address will be deleted.
